HERMES

Personal automation tool · Last updated November 23, 2026

HERMES is a private, personal automation tool used only by its owner to organize the owner's own Google Drive files. It is not offered to the public, and no one other than the owner can sign in to it.

Privacy Policy

HERMES accesses Google Drive and Google Sheets data only for the owner's own Google account, and only to read, organize, and store the owner's own files.

  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
  • No data is used for advertising.
  • Data stays in the owner's Google account and on the owner's private server.
  • HERMES's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Terms of Service

HERMES is provided for the owner's personal use only, as is, without warranty.
